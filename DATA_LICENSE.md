# Data and third-party rights

Source: DHI, *Inflow to a Waste Water Treatment Plant: observations and processed data*, Zenodo version 1, DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15301164. Source metadata records Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . Credit DHI and the Danish Meteorological Institute for their respective plant and weather observations.

Unmodified observations.zip and processed.zip are included with provider MD5 verification. Extracted CSVs and transformed hourly/target/prediction outputs identify their source and transformations in the methods and scripts. Preserve attribution and applicable non-commercial restrictions when reusing data. The upstream README has inconsistent BY/BY-NC prose; this release follows the more restrictive license explicitly recorded in the pinned Zenodo metadata rather than treating the data as unrestricted.

LICENSE applies only to newly authored analysis code. Third-party publications, metadata and source materials retain their own rights. No third-party full-text article is redistributed here. The dataset DOI is not a DOI for the code or manuscript.
